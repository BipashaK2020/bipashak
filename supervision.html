Graduate research

Team & Supervision

Research across speech, biomedical signals and machine learning systems.

As Graduate Research Coordinator in Deakin's School of Engineering and Deputy Director of the NSBE Research Lab, I contribute to doctoral supervision and the wider research community.

Mentoring and lab culture

I established a weekly research seminar series within the NSBE Research Lab, giving students a regular forum to present work in progress and receive feedback.

In 2026, I led a Three Minute Thesis training initiative for the School's higher-degree researchers, co-facilitated with three colleagues. The programme combined a presentation framework, insights from past winners, a cross-disciplinary academic panel and a live demonstration.

Current doctoral research

My current supervision spans four project areas:

  • Quantum machine learning for quantitative healthcare assessment.
  • AI and machine learning for biomedical signals and images.
  • Workload optimisation in cloud and edge computing.
  • Multimodal sensing of fatigue through sweat and gait biomarkers.
