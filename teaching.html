Teaching approach

In IoT Systems Engineering and Applied Signal Processing, students collect their own sensor data and carry it through processing, analysis and deployment using industry-standard software and hardware.

I use staged assessments, shared rubrics and feedforward feedback. Deakin's Learning Analytics for Teachers helps me monitor engagement and adapt teaching during the trimester. From 2026, high-weighted tasks include a live component in which students run and defend their work on their own data.

My approach to assessment flexibility uses evidence of progress to support students while maintaining academic standards.
