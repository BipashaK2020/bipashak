About the measurements

These are research demonstrations, not medical devices. Their estimates depend on recording conditions and device hardware and should not be used to diagnose or monitor a medical condition.

Recordings are processed in the browser by these demos. Microphone, camera and motion access are requested only when an instrument is started. Sensor access requires a supported device and a secure connection.

The speech-timing demonstration is related to the topographic prominence research; the browser implementation is not the validated clinical study system.
