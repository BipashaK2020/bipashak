Sustained vowel

Explore pitch and acoustic variation in a sustained vowel.

Take a breath, press start, and hold a steady "ah" for as long as you comfortably can. Recording stops by itself a second after you go quiet.

●--phonation time, s
◉--mean pitch, Hz
∿--jitter, %
≈--shimmer, %
◎--harmonics to noise, dB
∟--pitch spread, Hz

This demo estimates pitch and amplitude variation from 40-millisecond frames. The jitter and shimmer readouts are frame-based approximations, not equivalent to cycle-level clinical measurements. Microphone processing, background noise and the recording setup can affect every estimate.

Further reading: Maryn Y, Ysenbaert F, Zarowski A, Vanspauwen R. Mobile communication devices, ambient noise, and acoustic voice measures. Journal of Voice 2017, 31(2): 248.e11 to 248.e23, doi:10.1016/j.jvoice.2016.07.023. Jitter, shimmer and harmonics-to-noise follow the definitions used in Praat (Boersma 1993).

Research demonstration, not a medical device. Estimates depend on the device and recording conditions; they are not a diagnosis. Captured samples are processed in browser memory and are not uploaded by this demo. Built by Bipasha Kashyap, NSBE Lab, Deakin University.
