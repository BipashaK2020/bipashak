Fingertip pulse

Explore an optical pulse signal through your phone camera. This demo does not measure oxygen saturation.

Press start, allow the camera, then cover the rear camera lens and flash completely with a fingertip. Rest the finger, do not press.

●--heart rate, beats per minute
◉--beat-to-beat variability, ms
∿--beats counted
≈--signal quality

The camera records changes in light beneath your fingertip. The demo filters the average redness of each frame and estimates pulse timing from detected peaks. Movement, lighting and camera frame rate can distort the result. It does not measure blood pressure or oxygen saturation.

Further reading: Scully CG, Lee J, Meyer J, Gorbach AM, Granquist-Fraser D, Mendelson Y, Chon KH. Physiological parameter monitoring from optical recordings with a mobile phone. IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering 2012, 59(2): 303 to 306, doi:10.1109/TBME.2011.2163157.

Research demonstration, not a medical device. Estimates depend on the device and recording conditions; they are not a diagnosis. Captured samples are processed in browser memory and are not uploaded by this demo. Built by Bipasha Kashyap, NSBE Lab, Deakin University.
