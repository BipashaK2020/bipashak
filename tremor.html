Tremor spectrum

Hold the phone in an outstretched hand for ten seconds. The accelerometer does the rest.

Hold the phone flat in your outstretched hand, then press the button.

●--peak frequency, Hz
◉--movement, milli-g
∿--share in 3 to 7 Hz
≈--share in 7 to 12 Hz

The upper trace shows recorded movement and the lower trace shows its power spectrum. The 3 to 7 Hz and 7 to 12 Hz bands are shaded for comparison. Sampling rate depends on the device and browser. Frequency bands alone do not identify a tremor type or medical condition.

Further reading: Kostikis N, Hristu-Varsakelis D, Arnaoutoglou M, Kotsavasiloglou C. A smartphone-based tool for assessing parkinsonian hand tremor. IEEE Journal of Biomedical and Health Informatics 2015, 19(6): 1835 to 1842, doi:10.1109/JBHI.2015.2471093.

Research demonstration, not a medical device. Estimates depend on the device and recording conditions; they are not a diagnosis. Captured samples are processed in browser memory and are not uploaded by this demo. Built by Bipasha Kashyap, NSBE Lab, Deakin University.
